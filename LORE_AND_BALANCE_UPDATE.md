# P.A.C.K. HIGH COMMAND - ENGINEERING DIRECTIVE: INITIATIVE 0.9.8-b

**SECURITY CLASSIFICATION:** YELLOW-THREE
**AUTHOR:** Chief Engineer Silas Vance, Outpost 11
**SUBJECT:** Fleet-Wide Systems Standardization & Overhaul (Ref: "Operation Clean Slate")

Attention all P.A.C.K. line commanders and chief technicians:

Effective immediately, High Command is mandating a comprehensive retrofit across our primary combat formations. Our recent engagements have exposed several critical flaws in our jury-rigged subsystems and overly reckless power allocations. We are transitioning from unrefined brute force to sustained, calculated lethality. 

The following updates are to be pushed to all flight control networks and maintenance bays:

## 1. Tactical HUD & Hull Integrity Protocol
The days of duct-taped sensors and sparking conduit feeds are over. We have authorized the complete stripping of unstable visual artifacts that have been cluttering command feeds. 
* **REMOVED:** Obsolete floating text banners, erratic hull jitters, and hazardous external spark discharges.
* **IMPLEMENTED:** Clean, optimized weapon mount glows and high-definition tactical reticles. Your gunners will now see exactly where their ordnance is going without the distraction of our ships looking like they are tearing themselves apart.

## 2. Power Limiter Throttling (Tri-Overcharge & Ridgeback Drive)
Our previous specs pushed the Tri-Overcharge and Ridgeback Drive power limiters to a reckless 25%. While the burst output was spectacular, the resultant structural fatigue and flux blowbacks were costing us ships we cannot easily replace.
* **ADJUSTMENT:** Both systems have been hard-throttled down to a stable **15%**. 
* **RESULT:** This prevents catastrophic hull failure under sustained engagement and creates a perfectly sustainable, flux-neutral operating state. We are prioritizing longevity and sustained fire over suicidal alpha strikes.

## 3. Tactical Re-Classification & Resource Reallocation
In light of their extreme combat lethality and the increased maintenance burden of their advanced refits, several core chassis have been officially re-classified. Fleet quartermasters must adjust their Deployment Point (DP) budgets accordingly:
* **Ridgeback:** Re-classified to **9 DP**
* **Ridgeback X:** Re-classified to **11 DP**
* **Ridgeback X (RH):** Re-classified to **12 DP**
* **Komondor:** Re-classified to **14 DP**

These are no longer disposable assets. Treat them with the tactical respect their new designations demand.

## 4. Chassis Subsystem Integrations
To offset the power throttling and provide our brawlers with better point defense and burst capabilities, specific hulls have been outfitted with new integrated systems:
* **Bedlington:** Now features integrated **Canister Flak** to clear inbound fighter screens and missile swarms.
* **Bully Kutta:** Retrofitted with **Fast Missile Racks**, dramatically increasing its burst-damage potential and overwhelming enemy point defense.
* **Bulldog:** Upgraded with the **Alpha Call** system, allowing for unparalleled tactical coordination in the vanguard.

All shipyard chiefs are to begin these retrofits immediately. Any captain caught overriding the new 15% limiters will be personally answering to the tribunal. 

*Fight smart. Hit hard. Endure.*

**[END OF DIRECTIVE]**
